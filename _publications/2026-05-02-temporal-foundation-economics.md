*Volume I of the trilogy "Temporal Economics" (《经济学的时间的研究》).*

This volume develops the formal foundations of temporal economics. It introduces three core constructs:

- **Temporal feasibility** — the condition that any economic plan must be realizable within the temporal capacity of the agents and institutions that carry it out;
- **Temporal equilibrium** — the dynamic state in which the coordination capacity of the system matches the temporal demands placed upon it;
- **The shadow cost of time** — the price that economic systems implicitly pay when temporal constraints are violated, expressed through breakdowns in coordination, mistiming of decisions, and the erosion of institutional reliability.

By treating time as an endogenous and binding constraint rather than a passive backdrop, the book reframes long-standing problems in economic theory: the limits of optimization under finite coordination capacity, the structural sources of recurrent crises, and the conditions under which economic feasibility can be sustained.
